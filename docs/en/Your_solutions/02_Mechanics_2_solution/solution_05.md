# 🏃‍♂️ Collision Problem — Runner and Cart

## 📘 Problem

A **70 kg runner** moving at:

```
3 m/s
```

jumps onto a **140 kg cart** that is initially at rest.

Find:

1. Final speed of the cart with the runner  
2. Is kinetic energy conserved? Explain  

---

# 🧠 Step 1 — Understand the Type of Collision

This is an **inelastic collision** because:

```
runner + cart stick together
```

👉 Important:

- Momentum is conserved ✅  
- Kinetic energy is NOT conserved ❌  

---

# ⚡ Step 2 — Use Conservation of Momentum

Momentum formula:

$$
p = mv
$$

Total momentum before = total momentum after:

$$
m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2 = (m_1 + m_2)v_f
$$

---

# 🧮 Step 3 — Substitute Values

Runner:

```
m₁ = 70 kg
v₁ = 3 m/s
```

Cart:

```
m₂ = 140 kg
v₂ = 0
```

So:

$$
70 \cdot 3 + 140 \cdot 0 = (70 + 140)v_f
$$

---

# 🚀 Step 4 — Solve

$$
210 = 210 v_f
$$

$$
v_f = 1 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

# ✅ Final Speed

```
v = 1 m/s
```

---

# ⚡ Step 5 — Check Kinetic Energy

## Before collision

$$
KE_{before} = \frac12 m_1 v_1^2
$$

$$
KE_{before} = \frac12 (70)(3^2)
$$

$$
KE_{before} = 35 \cdot 9 = 315 \text{ J}
$$

---

## After collision

$$
KE_{after} = \frac12 (m_1 + m_2) v_f^2
$$

$$
KE_{after} = \frac12 (210)(1^2)
$$

$$
KE_{after} = 105 \text{ J}
$$

---

# 🔥 Step 6 — Compare Energies

```
Before: 315 J
After:  105 J
```

👉 Energy decreased!

---

# ❌ Is Kinetic Energy Conserved?

No.

```
Kinetic energy is NOT conserved
```

---

# 🧠 Why?

Because this is an **inelastic collision**:

- Objects stick together  
- Some energy turns into:
  - heat 🔥  
  - sound 🔊  
  - deformation  

---

# 🏁 Final Answers

### Final Speed

```
1 m/s
```

### Energy Conservation

```
Kinetic energy is NOT conserved
```

---

# 🎓 Key Concepts

Momentum:

$$
m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2 = (m_1 + m_2)v
$$

Kinetic energy:

$$
KE = \frac12 mv^2
$$
