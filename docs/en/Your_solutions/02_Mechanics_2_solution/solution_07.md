# 🧱 Friction Problem — Step by Step Solution

## 📘 Problem

A **5 kg block** is placed on a **10 kg block**.

- A horizontal force of **45 N** is applied to the **10 kg block**
- The **5 kg block is tied to a wall** (so it does NOT move)
- Coefficient of kinetic friction:

```
μ = 0.2
```

Find the **acceleration of the 10 kg block**

---

# 🧠 Step 1 — Understand the situation

We have:

- Bottom block (10 kg) → moves
- Top block (5 kg) → stays fixed (because tied)

👉 So there is **friction between blocks**

Also:

👉 Bottom block experiences **friction with ground**

---

# ⚡ Step 2 — Forces acting on 10 kg block

Forces on 10 kg block:

1. Applied force:

```
F = 45 N (to the right)
```

2. Friction from ground (opposes motion)

3. Friction from top block (also opposes motion)

👉 So total friction = TWO sources

---

# 📐 Step 3 — Normal Forces

### Ground normal force:

The ground supports BOTH masses:

```
N_ground = (10 + 5)g
```

```
N_ground = 15 × 9.8 = 147 N
```

---

### Normal between blocks:

Top block presses on bottom:

```
N_top = 5g = 49 N
```

---

# 🧮 Step 4 — Friction Forces

Friction formula:

$$
f = \mu N
$$

---

### Ground friction:

$$
f_{ground} = 0.2 × 147 = 29.4 N
$$

---

### Friction from top block:

$$
f_{top} = 0.2 × 49 = 9.8 N
$$

---

# 🔥 Step 5 — Total Opposing Force

Both frictions act against motion:

$$
F_{friction} = 29.4 + 9.8
$$

$$
F_{friction} = 39.2 N
$$

---

# 🚀 Step 6 — Net Force

$$
F_{net} = F_{applied} - F_{friction}
$$

$$
F_{net} = 45 - 39.2
$$

$$
F_{net} = 5.8 N
$$

---

# ⚡ Step 7 — Acceleration

Only the **10 kg block moves**, so:

$$
a = \frac{F_{net}}{m}
$$

$$
a = \frac{5.8}{10}
$$

$$
a = 0.58 \text{ m/s}^2
$$

---

# ✅ Final Answer

Acceleration of 10 kg block:

$$
a = 0.58 \text{ m/s}^2
$$

---

# 🎓 Key Ideas (Very Important)

- Friction acts wherever surfaces slide  
- Two friction forces here:
  - ground  
  - between blocks  
- Top block is fixed → creates friction on bottom block  
- Only bottom block accelerates
