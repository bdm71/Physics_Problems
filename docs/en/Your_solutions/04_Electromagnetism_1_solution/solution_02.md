# ⚡ Electric Potential at the Center of a Square – Step-by-Step Solution

Point charges of:

- +1 C  
- -2 C  
- +3 C  
- -4 C  

are placed at the corners of a square of side:

a = 1.0 m

(in order around the square)

We need to calculate:

### Electric potential at the center of the square

---

# 📘 Step 1: Formula for Electric Potential

The electric potential due to a point charge is:

V = kq / r

where:

- V = electric potential  
- k = 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²  
- q = charge  
- r = distance from charge to point  

Since potential is a scalar quantity, we simply **add all potentials algebraically**.

---

# 📘 Step 2: Distance from Corner to Center

For a square of side a:

Diagonal = a√2

Distance from center to any corner:

r = (a√2) / 2

Substitute a = 1.0 m:

r = √2 / 2 = 0.707 m

So each corner is the same distance from the center.

---

# 📘 Step 3: Add All Charges

Total charge:

q(total) = +1 + (-2) + 3 + (-4)

q(total) = 1 - 2 + 3 - 4

q(total) = -2 C

---

# 📘 Step 4: Total Potential

Since all charges are at the same distance:

V = k × q(total) / r

Substitute values:

V = (9 × 10⁹ × -2) / 0.707

V = -18 × 10⁹ / 0.707

V ≈ -2.55 × 10¹⁰ V

---

# ✅ Final Answer

**Electric potential at the center:**

V ≈ -2.55 × 10¹⁰ volts

---

# 📌 Final Summary

| Quantity | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Side of square | 1.0 m |
| Distance to center | 0.707 m |
| Total charge | -2 C |
| Electric potential | -2.55 × 10¹⁰ V |

---

# 🧠 Extra Note

- Electric potential is **scalar**, so we add directly.
- Electric field would require vector addition, but potential does not.
