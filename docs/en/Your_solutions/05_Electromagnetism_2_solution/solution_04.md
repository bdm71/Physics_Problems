# 🧲 Magnetic Torque on a Rectangular Current Loop

## 📌 Problem Statement

A rectangular loop of wire has dimensions:
- Length = 10 cm  
- Width = 5 cm  
- Current = 2 A  
- Magnetic field = 0.3 T  

The magnetic field is **parallel to the plane of the loop**.

👉 Find the magnitude of the magnetic torque.

---

## 🧠 Key Idea

A current loop in a magnetic field experiences a torque due to the interaction between:
- the magnetic field **B**
- the current **I**
- the area of the loop **A**

The torque depends on how the loop is oriented relative to the field.

---

## ⚙️ Formula

$$
\tau = N I A B \sin(\theta)
$$

Where:
- τ → torque (N·m)  
- N → number of turns (assume 1)  
- I → current (A)  
- A → area of loop (m²)  
- B → magnetic field (T)  
- θ → angle between **normal to loop** and magnetic field  

---

## 📐 Step 1: Convert Units

We convert everything into SI units:

- 10 cm = 0.10 m  
- 5 cm = 0.05 m  

---

## 📏 Step 2: Calculate Area

The loop is a rectangle:

$$
A = length \times width
$$

$$
A = 0.10 \times 0.05 = 0.005 \, m^2
$$

👉 This is the effective area of the loop.

---

## 📌 Step 3: Determine the Angle

- Magnetic field is **parallel to the plane of the loop**
- The normal vector is **perpendicular to the plane**

So:

- θ = 90°
- sin(θ) = 1

---

## ⚙️ Step 4: Substitute into Formula

$$
\tau = (1)(2)(0.005)(0.3)(1)
$$

---

## 🧮 Step 5: Step-by-step Calculation

First:
- 2 × 0.005 = 0.01  

Then:
- 0.01 × 0.3 = 0.003  

---

## ✅ Final Answer

$$
\tau = 3 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{N·m}
$$

or

**τ = 0.003 N·m**

---

## 🔍 Physical Insight

- Maximum torque happens when sin(θ) = 1  
- That is why this case gives the **maximum possible torque**
- If the field were perpendicular to the plane → torque would be zero

---

## 🚀 Summary

- Area is small → torque is small  
- Orientation gives maximum sin(θ)  
- Final torque depends linearly on I, A, and B  

---

## 🧲 Final Result

👉 **Magnetic Torque = 0.003 N·m**
