# Current from Charge Function

Given:

$$
Q(t) = 5t^2 + 5
$$

We are asked to find the **current at \( t = 3s \)**.

---

# Step 1: Key Physics Idea

Current is the **rate of change of charge with respect to time**:

$$
I(t) = \frac{dQ}{dt}
$$

---

# Step 2: Differentiate the Function

Given:

$$
Q(t) = 5t^2 + 5
$$

Differentiate:

- Derivative of \(5t^2\) is \(10t\)
- Derivative of constant \(5\) is \(0\)

So:

$$
I(t) = 10t
$$

---

# Step 3: Substitute \( t = 3 \)

$$
I(3) = 10 \cdot 3
$$

$$
I(3) = 30
$$

---

# Final Answer

$$
\boxed{I = 30 \text{ A}}
$$

---

# Quick Intuition

- Charge increases quadratically → current increases linearly  
- At \( t = 3s \), slope is \(30\), meaning **30 coulombs per second flow at that moment**
