# Average Current of a Lightning Bolt

Given:

- Charge transferred:

$$
Q = 30\text{ C}
$$

- Time interval:

$$
t = 2\text{ ms}
$$

Convert milliseconds to seconds:

$$
2\text{ ms} = 2 \times 10^{-3}\text{ s}
$$

$$
t = 0.002\text{ s}
$$

---

# Step 1: Use the Current Formula

Average current is given by:

$$
I = \frac{Q}{t}
$$

---

# Step 2: Substitute Values

$$
I = \frac{30}{0.002}
$$

---

# Step 3: Calculate

$$
I = 15000
$$

---

# Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
I = 15000\text{ A}
}
$$

---

# Quick Explanation

A lightning bolt transfers a huge amount of charge in a very tiny time.

Because the time is extremely small:

$$
0.002\text{ s}
$$

the current becomes extremely large:

$$
15000\text{ amperes}
$$
