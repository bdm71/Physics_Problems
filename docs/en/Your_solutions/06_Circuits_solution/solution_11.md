# Power and Energy in a Resistor

Given:

- Resistance:

$$
R = 100\Omega
$$

- Voltage:

$$
V = 50\text{ V}
$$

- Time:

$$
t = 5\text{ minutes}
$$

---

# Part 1: Find the Power Dissipated

## Step 1: Use the Power Formula

For a resistor:

$$
P = \frac{V^2}{R}
$$

---

## Step 2: Substitute Values

$$
P = \frac{50^2}{100}
$$

First calculate \(50^2\):

$$
50^2 = 2500
$$

Now substitute:

$$
P = \frac{2500}{100}
$$

---

## Step 3: Calculate

$$
P = 25
$$

---

# Power Answer

$$
\boxed{
P = 25\text{ W}
}
$$

---

# Part 2: Find the Energy Consumed in 5 Minutes

## Step 1: Convert Time to Seconds

$$
5\text{ minutes} = 5 \times 60
$$

$$
t = 300\text{ s}
$$

---

## Step 2: Use the Energy Formula

Energy:

$$
E = Pt
$$

---

## Step 3: Substitute Values

$$
E = 25 \times 300
$$

---

## Step 4: Calculate

$$
E = 7500
$$

---

# Final Energy Answer

$$
\boxed{
E = 7500\text{ J}
}
$$

---

# Final Answers

| Quantity | Result |
|---|---|
| Power dissipated | $$25\text{ W}$$ |
| Energy consumed in 5 minutes | $$7500\text{ J}$$ |

---

# Quick Explanation

- The resistor converts electrical energy into heat.
- Higher voltage produces more power.
- Energy depends on both power and time.
